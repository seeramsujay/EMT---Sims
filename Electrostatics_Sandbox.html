💡 How Electrostatics Works

1. Electric Charges ($q$) create an electric field $\vec{E}$ in surrounding space.
2. Positive charges ($+q$) push field lines outward; Negative charges ($-q$) pull field lines inward.
3. A test charge ($q_0$) experiences a Coulomb Force $\vec{F} = q_0 \vec{E}$ directed along the field vector.
4. Adjust charge magnitudes and drag the green test charge to observe how $\vec{E}$ and potential $V$ change in real time!
