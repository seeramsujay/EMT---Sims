💡 How Magnetism Works

1. Electric Currents ($I$) create magnetic fields $\vec{B}$ (Biot-Savart & Ampere's Laws).
2. Right-Hand Rule: Point thumb in current direction $I$, fingers curl in direction of $\vec{B}$ field.
3. Magnetic field lines $\vec{B}$ form continuous closed loops with zero divergence ($\nabla \cdot \vec{B} = 0$).
4. Moving charges experience Lorentz Force $\vec{F}_m = q (\vec{v} \times \vec{B})$ perpendicular to both velocity and field!
