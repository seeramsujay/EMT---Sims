LEC 03 E-Field Simulator

Electrostatics

Coulomb's law, point charges (+q / -q) placement in 3D space, net Coulomb force, and electric field vector field.

• F = (q1 q2)/(4π ε0 r²) ar
• E = q/(4π ε0 r²) ar (V/m)
• Superposition: E_net = Σ E_i

• ∮ E · dS = Q_enc / ε0
• D = ε0 E
• ∇ · D = ρv

LEC 05 Dielectric Engine

Dielectric Polarization

Microscopic dipole alignment in external electric field E0, polarization vector P, and bound surface charge density σb.

• P = χe ε0 E
• D = ε0 E + P = ε0 εr E
• σb = P · n

LEC 06 Potential Engine

Potential & Work

3D equipotential contours V(r), test charge trajectory path integration A → B, and E-field gradient relationship E = -∇V.

• W = -q ∫ E · dl = q(VB - VA)
• V(r) = q / (4π ε0 r)
• E = -∇V

LEC 07 2D FDM Grid Solver

Poisson & Laplace Solver

Interactive 2D/3D Finite Difference Method (FDM) relaxation grid solver for electrostatics boundary value problems.

• Laplace: ∇²V = 0
• Poisson: ∇²V = -ρ / ε
• FDM: V_ij = ¼(V_u + V_d + V_l + V_r)

LEC 08 Boundary Engine

Electrostatic Boundaries

Dielectric-dielectric and conductor interfaces showing tangential continuity Et1 = Et2 and law of refraction tan(θ1)/tan(θ2) = ε1/ε2.

• Et1 = Et2 (Continuous)
• Dn1 - Dn2 = σs (Jump)
• tan(θ1)/tan(θ2) = ε1/ε2

LEC 09 Magnetic Field Simulator

Magnetostatics

3D current carrying wire, concentric magnetic flux density B loops, Biot-Savart law, and Ampere's closed loop line integral.

• dB = (μ0 I dl × ar) / (4π r²)
• ∮ H · dl = I_enc
• ∇ · B = 0

LEC 10 Magnetic Boundary Engine

Magnetic Boundaries

Magnetic media interfaces (μ1, μ2) with surface currents K, demonstrating Bn continuity and tangential H discontinuity.

• Bn1 = Bn2 (Continuous)
• Ht1 - Ht2 = K (Discontinuous)
• tan(θ1)/tan(θ2) = μ1/μ2
Launch 3D Simulation 🚀
LEC 11 3D TEM Wave Engine

Maxwell & EM Waves

3D Transverse Electromagnetic (TEM) wave propagation: orthogonal E(z,t) and H(z,t) waves, Faraday induction, and displacement current.

• ∇ × E = -∂B/∂t
• ∇ × H = J + ∂D/∂t
• c = 1 / √(μ0 ε0)
