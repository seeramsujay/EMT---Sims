⛰️ Gradient (∇V) — The Steepest Hill Slope

Intuition: Imagine standing on a 3D mountain landscape of Electric Potential $V(x,y,z)$. The Gradient $\nabla V$ points in the direction of the steepest ascent (uphill), and its magnitude tells you how steep the slope is.

Why in Physics? Electric charges want to roll downhill from high voltage to low voltage. That's why Electric Field is the negative gradient: $\vec{E} = -\nabla V$!
